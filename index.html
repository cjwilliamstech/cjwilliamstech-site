About

I'm an engineer at heart,
who loves to build things.

I'm the kind of person who has a project on the bench at all times. Right now it's a HackerBox shortwave radio build — ESP32-C3, RF, hand-assembled antennas, and a crash course in the black magic of the Smith Chart. It's a natural fit: I'm a licensed amateur radio operator (Technician class), so the airwaves are already home turf. Before the radio it was drones. Next it'll be something else. I learn by taking things apart and putting them back together better.

Professionally, that same instinct is pointed at Identity & Access Management. I'm a BeyondTrust-certified IAM engineer with hands-on experience running enterprise identity infrastructure at a major utility operating at scale — an environment where identity security isn't optional and getting it wrong has real consequences.

The two sides feed each other. The tinkering keeps me curious and unafraid of new hardware, new APIs, new problems. The IAM work keeps me disciplined about security, auditability, and doing things properly. Lately I've been merging them — building AI-assisted identity tooling and pointing AI vision at the things I fly.
